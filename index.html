The Origin

“Francis, reimagined. Nova, a star newly born. Together, a house built on the belief that every legend deserves a second first drive.”

The answer is a collection of six vehicles — each one a familiar silhouette, completely reborn — engineered in-house, badge-swapped in spirit, and delivered under a single promise of craftsmanship.

The Founder

About Me

Francis Carl F. Dela Cruz

CEO & Founder of FRANOVA

Francis didn't start in a boardroom — he started in a cramped garage with a secondhand toolbox and a stubborn belief that great engineering shouldn't stay locked behind old names. He spent his early years sketching silhouettes on scrap paper, taking apart engines just to understand why they roared the way they did, and getting turned away from investors who couldn't see past his age.

Instead of chasing approval, he built. One redesigned chassis became a prototype. One prototype became a small workshop. That workshop became FRANOVA — a house built from the ground up on the idea that every legend deserves a second first drive, and that a new beginning, done right, can outshine even the oldest star. Today, Francis leads FRANOVA the same way he built it: hands-on, relentless, and still sketching silhouettes on scrap paper when no one's watching.
